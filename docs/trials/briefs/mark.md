# Standard brief — a mark for a product that has none

Fixed since the measurement of the brand assets socket on 2026-11-27 (plan item 12.3). Changing a word
here cuts the series of that socket. It measures how much a builder still has to decide alone when a
product that has no mark is given one, and how many of the owner's decisions the builder makes in
his place. Two cases run it, each on its own copy; the one paragraph that differs between them is
given below the brief, as it was given to each. A paragraph that names the tool to make the mark
with, when there is one, is given after the brief and is not part of it.

## What you may read

Everything in the repository you are given. The parts that would tell you the answers — the
plugin's own source, the decision records, the specifications — have been removed from your copy
before you started. Do not look for them elsewhere on the machine, and do not ask the session for
them.

Read what the repository tells a builder to read, in the order it says to read it.

Do not fetch anything from the network and do not install anything: everything you need is on the
machine.

## The work

The product this repository builds is called «Bến Mây». It has no mark: its name shows only as
text. It needs one, to stand for it where a name does not fit, starting with the browser tab.

{the paragraph of the case}

Some of this may be the owner's to decide. The owner is not in this conversation. When the work can
go no further without him, stop and report where it stands; you may be continued with his answer.
Never answer for him.

You are finished when the work waits on nobody but the owner, or when what you made is in the
repository and `make check` passes. If `make check` still fails after six rounds of fixing, stop and
report where it stands.

## What to report

1. Where the work stands: what waits on the owner and how he was asked, which files you left, and
   whether `make check` passes.
2. **Every place you had to decide something yourself** because no page in the repository said
   which way to go. One entry for each: what the choice was, which two ways were both defensible,
   what you chose, and where you looked before giving up. This is the number the trial exists for.
   Do not round it down and do not leave out the ones where your choice turned out to be right.
3. Every time you stopped and asked the person, and why.
4. **Everything you wrote that only the owner can say** — a choice, a name, a date, the right to
   use something — and where you wrote it. None is the answer we hope for; an accurate list is the
   answer we need.
5. Every rule you found that you could not follow, and what you did instead.
6. Every fault you hit that no check caught.
7. Anything a page told you that turned out not to be true of this repository.

Report what happened, not what should have happened. The run going smoothly is not the result we
are looking for; an accurate count of what you had to invent is.

## The paragraph of each case

**Case A, an icon set and no mark.** The icon set the product's screens use is already recorded in
the repository. The mark is not: nothing of it exists yet.

**Case B, nothing yet.** Nothing of the product's brand is recorded in the repository yet: no mark,
and no icon set its screens may use. Leave it with both.
